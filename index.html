No Student Left Behind

"Your results do not define your future. Every Sri Lankan student has a unique set of skills and a path to a bright career."

EduPath Lanka is built on the core belief that exams like O/L and A/L are checkpoints, not endpoints. If your results weren't what you hoped for, or if you feel uncertain about your stream, there are thousands of non-traditional options, vocational training, international certifications, and scholarships waiting for you.

Financial Support for Your Dreams

Money should not be a barrier to education. We aggregate and guide you through various funding options available in Sri Lanka and abroad.

  • Government Scholarships: Mahapola, Bursaries, and Presidential Funds.
  • NGO Support: Grants for rural and underprivileged students.
  • Free Courses: High-quality online courses with free certificates.
  • Student Loans: Low-interest educational loan schemes.
  • Low-cost Learning: Subsidized vocational training (NVQ).
