About EduPath Lanka

Guiding Sri Lankan students towards the right education, skills, mentors, and career opportunities.

EduPath Lanka was created to help students who feel confused after O/L or A/L examinations. The platform provides guidance, personalized pathways, mentorship, scholarships, and career awareness so that every student can find a meaningful future path.

Why EduPath Lanka Was Created

Many Sri Lankan students feel lost after major exams. Some do not qualify for university, some fail exams, some cannot afford higher studies, and some do not know about alternative paths such as diplomas, vocational training, online learning, freelancing, entrepreneurship, and professional courses.

We realized there was a huge gap between the incredible potential of our youth and the clear guidance needed to unlock it. EduPath Lanka was born out of the necessity to bridge this gap, ensuring that every student has access to personalized, clear, and actionable career advice regardless of their academic background.

Our Mission

To guide every Sri Lankan student toward suitable education and career opportunities based on their results, skills, interests, and financial background.

Our Vision

To build a future where no student in Sri Lanka feels left behind after exams.
