What is Pathway Finder?

Pathway Finder is a guided profile-building tool that helps students choose a suitable future direction.

It supports students who want to

  • Continue academic studies
  • Develop talents
  • Combine education and talents
  • Improve academic skills first
  • Explore options when they are undecided

After completing the form, registered students can receive relevant recommendations including courses, scholarships, mentors, institutes and talent opportunities.

Choose the Path That Matches You

Your form changes to collect only the details relevant to your direction.

Academic Path

For students who want to build their future through education, qualifications, courses and scholarships.

Courses, scholarships, institutes, academic mentors and career mentors.

Talent Path

For students growing through dancing, music, sports, art, design, media, photography or practical skills.

Talent opportunities, coaches, academies, talent scholarships and skill-building programs.

Combined Path

For students who want to continue education while developing their talents.

Academic and talent opportunities together.

Undecided Path

For students who are still exploring and need guidance to choose a direction.

Discovery questions, beginner courses, general scholarships and guidance mentors.

How Pathway Finder Works

1

2

3

4

5

Save opportunities, request mentors and update your pathway anytime.

What Details Will You Add?

You only need to fill the sections relevant to your selected path.

Academic Path

  • Education level and available O/L or A/L background
  • Preferred academic fields and career goals
  • Study preferences and financial support needs

Talent Path

  • Talent category, skill level and experience
  • Optional achievements and portfolio links
  • Opportunity, mentor or coach preferences

Combined Path

  • Academic details
  • Talent details
  • Future goals connecting both

Undecided Path

  • Interests and favorite activities
  • Preferred learning style and skills to improve
  • Support needed
